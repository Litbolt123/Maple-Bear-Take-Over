When Minecraft updated to version 1.13, a changed dubbed "The Flattening" was introduced. In "The Flattening", numerical IDs and item data was removed, and some item IDs were changed to make them more logical - Hay Bale was one of the many items that had their IDs changed.

## Hay Bale Information

![Hay Bale](https://static.minecraftitemids.com/64/hay_block.png)

A hay bale can be mined with any tool, or your hand and can be crafted by placing wheat in each square (9) of a crafting bench. They can be fed to animals such as horses in Minecraft.
