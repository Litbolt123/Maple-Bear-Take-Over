**Hay bales** are storage [blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block "Block") equivalent to nine pieces of [wheat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wheat "Wheat"). They are used as a [crafting](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting "Crafting") ingredient, to feed [llamas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Llama "Llama") and all [horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Horse "Horse") variants, reduce [fall damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Fall_damage "Damage"), and extend [campfire](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire "Campfire") smoke.

### Natural generation

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Natural generation") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Natural generation")\]

Hay bales often generate as [piles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pile "Pile") (and in some houses, animal pens, farms, or meeting points) scattered around in [villages](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village "Village") located in [plains](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Plains "Plains"), and less commonly in [savanna](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Savanna "Savanna") and [desert](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Desert "Desert") villages. They also can generate under some [campfires](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire "Campfire") in [taiga](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Taiga "Taiga") villages and as a part of targets/scarecrows in [pillager outposts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_outpost "Pillager outpost").

Hay bales can also generate in [abandoned camps](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Abandoned_camp "Abandoned camp"). ​\[ _[upcoming](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Planned_versions "Planned versions")_\]

## Usage

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Usage") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Usage")\]

Hay bales can be used as compact storage of [wheat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wheat "Wheat").

### Placement

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=6&veaction=edit "Edit section: Placement") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section's source code: Placement")\]

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Orientables.png/300px-Orientables.png?efde1)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Orientables.png) Seen here alongside [logs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Log "Log"), [bone blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_block "Bone block"), [quartz pillars](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Quartz_pillar "Quartz pillar"), [purpur pillars](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Purpur_pillar "Purpur pillar"), [polished basalt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polished_basalt "Polished basalt"), and [barrels](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Barrel "Barrel"), hay bales can be pointed in multiple directions.

Hay bales can be placed pointing in all three spatial dimensions, in the same way as a log is placed. However, there is no "six-sided" variant like that of the [wood](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wood "Wood") block.

### Food

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=8&veaction=edit "Edit section: Food") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section's source code: Food")\]

Although players cannot eat hay bales, hay bales serve as a compact wheat supply for crafting food items during long periods away from other food sources. A full stack of 64 hay bales is equivalent to 192 [bread](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bread "Bread").

### Animals

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=9&veaction=edit "Edit section: Animals") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section's source code: Animals")\]

Hay bales can be fed to [donkeys](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Donkey "Donkey"), [horses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Horse "Horse"), [llamas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Llama "Llama"), or [mules](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mule "Mule") to heal up to 10 hearts. They are an effective method to heal horses if the player wants to heal them in a short period of time. They also speed up the growth of foals by three minutes and baby llamas by 90 seconds. In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition"), due to a bug, [skeleton horses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_horse "Skeleton horse") (in their hostile state; being ridden by [skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")) can also be fed hay bales.[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale#cite_note-2)

#### Breeding

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=10&veaction=edit "Edit section: Breeding") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section's source code: Breeding")\]

Hay bales can be used both to lead and [breed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeding "Breeding") llamas.

### Falling

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=11&veaction=edit "Edit section: Falling") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=11 "Edit section's source code: Falling")\]

Falling onto a hay bale reduces the [fall damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Fall_damage "Damage") by 80%, meaning whatever falls on a hay bale takes 20% of the normal fall damage.

Below are some example damages a player receives falling on a hay bale (assuming no enchantments and/or status effects that reduce fall damage).

| Fall height | Fall damage |
| --- | --- |
| 8-12 blocks | 1HP![💔](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Half_Heart_%28icon%29.png?cb16e) |
| 13-17 blocks | 2HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) |
| 98-102 blocks | 19HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) × 9.5 |
| 103-107 blocks | 20HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) × 10 |
| 118-123 blocks | 23HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) × 11.5 |

Falling on a hay bale examples

Thus, it is possible to fall onto a hay bale and survive a 100 block drop (starting with full health) or even a 120 block drop (starting with full health plus 2 [Absorption](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Absorption "Absorption") hearts).

### Campfires

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=12&veaction=edit "Edit section: Campfires") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=12 "Edit section's source code: Campfires")\]

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Campfire_with_smoke.gif?cd428)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Campfire_with_smoke.gif) A campfire emitting smoke

Placing a hay bale under a [campfire](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire "Campfire") makes it a signal fire, increasing the height to which its smoke particles can rise from 10 blocks to 25 blocks.

### Composting

\[ [edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?section=13&veaction=edit "Edit section: Composting") \| [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale?action=edit&section=13 "Edit section's source code: Composting")\]

Placing a hay bale into a [composter](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Composter "Composter") has an 85% chance of raising the compost level by 1. Composting with hay bales is very inefficient, as the hay bale's chance to be composted is only slightly higher than wheat. For example, 900 wheat could be composted to yield 834⁄7 [bone meal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_meal "Bone meal") on average, but if crafted into 100 hay bales, they would only yield 121⁄7 bone meal on average.
